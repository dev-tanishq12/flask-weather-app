Features

Everything you need
to plan your day

From real-time conditions to week-long outlooks and live air quality — WeatherPro puts it all in one beautiful dashboard.

Real-time Updates

Minute-by-minute data pulled from OpenWeatherMap's global satellite network — always accurate, never stale.

7-Day Forecast

Plan a full week ahead with highs, lows, precipitation chances, wind speed, and humidity at a glance.

Live Air Quality

Know the air you breathe before you step outside. Real-time AQI with PM2.5 and PM10 particle readings.

Temperature Trends

Beautiful interactive charts that visualise how temperatures evolve across the week — in °C or °F.

Saved Cities

Bookmark your favourite cities and switch between them in one click — perfect for travellers and remote workers.

GPS Auto-detect

One tap and your exact GPS coordinates are used to fetch hyper-local weather — no typing, no guessing.
